A wheelchair-accessible taxi can make a planned trip easier by giving families a way to travel together without treating every hospital journey as an ambulance ride.

## What trips can a wheelchair taxi help with?

A wheelchair cab is intended for non-emergency travel. Families commonly need a vehicle for a scheduled consultation, a follow-up visit, diagnostic appointments, physiotherapy, dialysis, or a planned hospital discharge when the passenger is stable and does not need clinical monitoring on the way.

The right transport depends on the passenger's condition. If someone needs oxygen, medical supervision, or urgent care while travelling, arrange an ambulance instead. A taxi provides transportation, not medical treatment.

## Book with the details that matter

When requesting a wheelchair taxi in Chennai, include:

- Exact pickup and drop-off addresses, including the hospital building or gate if known.
- Appointment time and your preferred pickup time.
- Whether the journey is one-way or a return trip.
- Wheelchair type and, if available, its dimensions and approximate weight.
- Whether the passenger will remain seated in the wheelchair during travel.
- Number of accompanying family members or caregivers.
- Any walking aid, luggage, waiting-time request, or special pickup instruction.

These details help the booking team check vehicle suitability and provide a trip-specific fare before you confirm. Do not assume that every wheelchair has the same dimensions or boarding requirements.

## Planning for dialysis and recurring appointments

Dialysis and rehabilitation can mean travelling on a regular schedule. Ask about recurring bookings early and provide the dates and appointment times you know. Discuss whether you need a return pickup, a second ride later, or waiting time at the clinic; these arrangements should be confirmed for your individual schedule.

Keep the booking contact number available on the day of travel. If the appointment time changes, tell the team as soon as possible so the pickup plan can be reviewed. For ongoing visits, retain your confirmed dates and route details in one place to make repeat booking easier.
